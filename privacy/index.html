Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 13, 2026

PineToast is an AI agent governance and control software product operated by Paper Wrench ("PineToast", "Paper Wrench", "we", "our", or "us").

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you use PineToast, including its policy, approval, access-control, execution, audit, monitoring, and connected-system features.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use PineToast, we may collect:

  • Account information such as your name, email address, workspace details, and authentication information.
  • Organization information such as workspace name, team membership, roles, policies, approval rules, agent configurations, and governance settings.
  • Information relating to AI agents, automations, workflows, tools, skills, models, or external systems that you register with PineToast.
  • Permission and access-control information, including allowed actions, restricted actions, credentials metadata, scopes, roles, and policy decisions.
  • Approval information, including approval requests, decisions, reviewers, timestamps, and related execution context.
  • Execution information, including requested actions, policy evaluations, execution results, receipts, errors, retries, and verification outcomes.
  • Usage and spend information, including usage limits, budgets, model or tool usage, and other cost-control data where those features are enabled.
  • Information received from connected business systems where you explicitly authorize PineToast or an integrated execution system to access that data.
  • Technical information such as browser information, IP address, logs, device information, security events, error reports, and usage telemetry.

2. Connected Systems and Integrations

PineToast may integrate with third-party services such as AI providers, email platforms, productivity tools, CRM systems, payment providers, developer tools, cloud services, data systems, and other applications.

The information available to PineToast depends on the integration, the permissions granted, and the functionality enabled by the user.

PineToast is designed to request or use only the access needed for supported features and authorized actions.

3. LinkedIn Integration

If LinkedIn functionality is enabled through PineToast or through another Paper Wrench product governed by PineToast, information may be processed through LinkedIn's authorized APIs and the permissions approved by the user.

Depending on the applicable integration and permissions, this may include:

  • LinkedIn member or organization identifiers.
  • Profile or organization information where permitted.
  • Information required to evaluate or authorize LinkedIn-related actions.
  • Publishing or execution receipts where supported.
  • Authentication or authorization metadata required to maintain access.

PineToast does not obtain or store a user's LinkedIn password.

LinkedIn actions remain subject to LinkedIn's terms, API policies, permissions, and technical limitations.

4. AI Agent and Workflow Data

PineToast may process information relating to AI agents and workflows in order to evaluate whether a proposed action is permitted.

This may include:

  • The identity of the requesting agent or service.
  • The requested tool, resource, or external system.
  • The proposed action and relevant parameters.
  • The applicable policy or rule.
  • Approval requirements.
  • Budget or usage constraints.
  • Execution and verification outcomes.

PineToast may retain audit records of these events for security, accountability, compliance, troubleshooting, and customer governance purposes.

5. How We Use Information

We may use collected information to:

  • Provide and operate PineToast.
  • Authenticate users and manage workspaces.
  • Evaluate policies and permissions.
  • Route and manage approvals.
  • Control access to tools, systems, data, and actions.
  • Enforce usage, budget, and spend limits.
  • Record execution receipts and audit trails.
  • Verify whether requested actions completed successfully.
  • Detect abuse, fraud, security threats, and policy violations.
  • Provide monitoring, observability, troubleshooting, and support.
  • Improve product reliability and functionality.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

6. Automated Policy Decisions

PineToast may automatically evaluate requests against policies, permissions, budgets, scopes, and approval requirements configured by the user.

These evaluations may result in an action being allowed, denied, delayed, escalated for approval, or otherwise restricted.

Users are responsible for configuring policies appropriate to their organization, risk profile, and intended use.

7. Human Approval

Where a policy requires human approval, PineToast may route the request to an authorized reviewer before allowing execution to proceed.

Approval records may be stored as part of the audit trail.

8. Credentials and Tokens

PineToast may process or store credentials, tokens, secrets, or authorization metadata when required to support connected systems.

Where supported, sensitive credentials are intended to be stored using appropriate security controls and should only be accessible to components that require them for authorized execution.

Users should avoid submitting credentials through ordinary text fields unless the product explicitly identifies the field as intended for secrets or credentials.

We do not sell personal information.

We may share information with service providers that help us operate PineToast, including providers of:

  • Cloud infrastructure and hosting.
  • Database and storage services.
  • Authentication and identity services.
  • Artificial intelligence services.
  • Monitoring and security services.
  • Communication and notification services.
  • Connected business systems selected by users.
  • Payment and billing services where applicable.

We may also disclose information when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect users, Paper Wrench, third parties, or the security of the service.
